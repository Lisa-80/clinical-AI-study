Study Information

Purpose

This study examines how AI-generated diagnostic support influences clinicians' diagnostic reasoning and clinical decision-making. Participants will review clinical case handoffs and provide diagnostic and management recommendations.

If you agree to participate, you will complete a web-based research study involving a series of clinical cases. For each case, you will provide diagnostic impressions, recommended tests, management plans, and disposition decisions. You will also complete brief questionnaires regarding your clinical experience and perspectives on medical decision-making.

The study is expected to take approximately 30 minutes to complete.

Voluntary Participation

Participation is voluntary. You may stop at any time without penalty.

Risks and Benefits

This study involves minimal risk. While you may not directly benefit, your participation may help improve understanding of how AI-generated diagnostic support affects clinical decision-making.

Confidentiality

Your study responses will be stored securely and analyzed using a study ID rather than your email address. Only authorized members of the research team will have access to study data.

Compensation

Upon completion of the survey, you will receive a $100 electronic gift card.

To receive compensation, you will be asked to provide an email address at the end of the study.

Clinical Case Review Study

Thank you for participating in this study.

You are a clinician evaluating a patient who completed an initial intake assessment using an AI-based system. The summary of that discussion with a link to the chatlog will be provided. The patient is at home. Based on the information provided, you will formulate a possible diagnosis & recommend next steps in the patient's care.

For each case, you should:

  • Provide possible diagnosis(es)
  • Recommend diagnostic testing (if any)
  • Recommend treatment and management (if any)
  • Determine the appropriate level of follow-up care

Your goal is to provide the best possible care at this point using only the information available in the case. Assume that you can recommend any options.

Please do not use any external resources while completing the study (e.g., AI tools, UpToDate, clinical references, search engines, or other decision-support resources).

This study is designed to be completed on a desktop or laptop computer or tablet. Do not attempt to complete the study on a mobile phone.

The AI information presented during the study may vary across participants as part of the study design.
